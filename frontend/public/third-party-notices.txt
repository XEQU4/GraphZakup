- One pending animation frame per pointer movement; no idle animation loop.
- Cancellation/reset on pointer leave/cancel, disabled motion, hidden document,
  offscreen state and unmount.
- Keyboard focus works without pointer input; native focus outline remains when
  decorative motion is disabled.
- No external asset, package installation or canvas/WebGL runtime.
